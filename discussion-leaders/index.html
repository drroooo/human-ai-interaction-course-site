    Discussion Leaders¶

    Goals¶

    Discussion Leaders are responsible for reading all assigned articles closely, critically evaluating them, and leading the class discussion. This is not a simple article-summary assignment. Because everyone is expected to complete the readings before class, Discussion Leaders should offer their own critiques of the articles' arguments, methods, and conclusions and organize a discussion of their broader implications. Leaders are encouraged to prepare a one-page discussion brief to distribute and use while facilitating the class.

    How to Do This Well¶

    1. Reading summary: Briefly introduce each article's hypotheses or research questions, central claims, key concepts and theoretical framework, methods, and findings.
    2. Reading critique: Present your own original critique of the articles' central concepts, theories, and methods. Explain what each study does particularly well and where it falls short. Consider whether the research design is appropriate, whether the concepts are measured convincingly, whether alternative explanations are possible, and whether the findings are likely to generalize or depend on a particular context.
    3. Reading connection: Discuss how the questions raised by the articles are connected and how their approaches are similar or different. Explain why these relationships matter for Human-AI Interaction. Based on the readings, identify questions that the field might develop or investigate next.
    4. Questions for class: Prepare creative and engaging questions that can move between personal experiences or concrete cases and scholarly concepts or theories. You may also organize this part as a short class activity.
    5. Don't be an AI copy machine: Do not copy and paste what an AI system produces. AI use is not prohibited, but AI should help you develop your thinking rather than replace it. The analysis, critique, and intellectual direction of the discussion must be your own.

    In-Class Roles¶

    • Provide any additional background knowledge that classmates need in order to understand the readings or discussion.
    • Ask interesting and useful follow-up questions and actively invite participation. You may call on particular students to respond.
    • When discussion remains too heavily focused on personal experiences or impressions and drifts away from scholarly analysis, help redirect it toward the readings, concepts, and evidence.

    Indicators of Strong Performance¶

    A strong Discussion Leader does more than understand the articles accurately. They connect and synthesize the readings, contribute an original critical perspective, and lead a rigorous discussion grounded in evidence. The number of slides or length of the presentation matters less than how effectively the leader helps the class think and engage at a deeper level.
